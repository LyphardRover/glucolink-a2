The matching stage produced 17 golden patient records and 46 LINKED source records. Two source records were deliberately left unresolved: LAB PATH0006 requires review because it has two candidates; PHARM PH-1012 has no candidate and remains UNMATCHED. Both have NULL patient_id and match_rule, so neither is silently assigned to a patient. These are synthetic fixture findings, not clinical conclusions.

The log lines saying “business findings reported separately” refer to this section and the acceptance results above. The test did not yet calculate the final UC1–UC4 business outputs.

## How the run was controlled

The eight unmodified source files were pinned to the commit above. The runner set the effective team role, stopped at the first SQL error (`ON_ERROR_STOP`), and used a 60-second statement timeout and 5-second lock timeout. Each round was followed by the same acceptance queries. No individual execution error was suppressed. Per-script logs and source checksums are retained privately.

Two successful executions establish repeatability for this scope; they do not prove every business rule or failure path. Scripts 07–10, final facts, four reporting views, charts, Appendix C and the complete end-to-end rebuild still require their owners’ final scripts and integration checks. The RAW comparison is read-only and does not test rollback fault injection.
